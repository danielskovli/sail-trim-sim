    How it works

    The dashed lines on the boat show where the coach would put each sail. Trim so the angle of attack sits just below stall (~20–25°) upwind and ease to the shrouds downwind. Reef when the predicted heel passes ~24°. The boat is a generic 40 ft cruising sloop: 45 m² main with three reefs, a 50 m² furling genoa, a working jib, a storm jib, a code 0 and asymmetric and symmetric spinnakers. Each downwind sail has a wind-angle envelope and a wind-speed limit; outside them it collapses or becomes dangerous, and the coach says so.

    Everything on the chart is draggable; the sliders remain for fine adjustment. Keys: ←/→ heading, ↑/↓ wind, [/] main sheet, ,/. genoa sheet, n new scenario, c check. Hold Shift for bigger steps.
